# Publicly available books, in learning order

These are author/publisher-hosted reading sources. Free online reading does not always mean permission to mirror or redistribute the book. Links are provided rather than unauthorized copies. Full books below were checked at their official landing pages on 2026-10-07; the probability book is linked from its author’s course page.

| ID | Book and legal reading source | Use it when | What to read / do | Access and caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B01 | [Think Python, third edition — Allen Downey](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkPython/) | M01 | In chapter order, with notebook exercises: variables, functions, conditions, iteration, collections, files and classes | Free author-hosted HTML/notebooks; suitable for first-time programmers |
| B02 | [Python for Data Analysis, third edition — Wes McKinney](https://wesmckinney.com/book/) | M04 | NumPy, pandas, cleaning, joins, aggregation, time series; reproduce labs on your own data | Free open HTML edition, updated for pandas 2.0; author restricts copying book content; code MIT |
| B03 | [Mathematics for Machine Learning — Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong](https://mml-book.github.io/) | M05 | Linear algebra, geometry, decompositions, vector calculus, probability and optimization; solve exercises | Free author-hosted PDF; needs the school-algebra bridge |
| B04 | [Introduction to Probability, second edition — Blitzstein, Hwang](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/stat110/home) | M06 | Use the free book link on Stat 110; conditioning, distributions, expectation and practice problems | Course author explicitly links a free online edition; no paid certificate needed |

## How much to read

Keep one core book open per stage: B01 → B02 → B03/B04 → B05 → B06 → B08 → B09. B07 is for depth; B10 is an implementation companion. Reading all books cover to cover before building would delay useful practice.

For research specialization, B12 adds optimization depth after the mathematical foundation. An attempted RL textbook landing page returned 404 and is recorded in the audit; it is not presented as a verified download. The official HF RL course remains available.

For each chapter keep one page: definitions in your words, one worked example, one exercise result, one connection to your project, and one unresolved question.
